DropToDocs Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

In short

  • Your files go straight from your browser to your own Google Drive. They never pass through any server run by DropToDocs.
  • DropToDocs can only see files it created for you. It cannot see anything else in your Drive.
  • There are no analytics, no tracking, and no ads. Your data is not sold or shared.

What DropToDocs does

DropToDocs is a browser extension. When you choose or drop Word, Excel or PowerPoint files (.doc, .docx, .xls, .xlsx, .ppt, .pptx), it uploads them to your Google Drive (into a folder of your choice, or the top level), asks Google to convert them to Google Docs, Sheets or Slides, and, if you choose, opens them in new tabs.

Information the extension uses

  • Your Google account email address. Used only to show which account you are signed in with, and to sign you back in without asking again. It is stored in your browser's extension storage on your device.
  • A temporary Google access token. Lets the extension upload files to your Drive on your behalf. It lasts about an hour, is kept only in your browser's session storage, and is erased when you sign out or close the browser.
  • The files you choose. Read only at the moment you select or drop them, and sent directly to Google Drive. DropToDocs does not keep a copy.
  • Your Google storage usage. How much of your Google storage is used and available, read from Google Drive only to display it in the popup. It is not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Your settings. Whether to open files after upload, where to save them, and the ID of the Drive folder DropToDocs created for you. Stored in your browser's extension storage so the popup remembers your choices.

DropToDocs does not collect your browsing history, the contents of web pages, your location, or any other personal information.

Google permissions requested

  • openid and email: to know which Google account you signed in with.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file: to create files and folders in your Google Drive. This permission only gives access to files and folders that DropToDocs itself creates or that you open with it. It does not allow reading, changing or deleting your other Drive files.

Where your data goes

The extension talks only to Google: accounts.google.com to sign in, and googleapis.com to upload files, read your email address, and read your storage usage. DropToDocs has no servers of its own and receives none of your data. The documents it creates belong to you and are covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Sharing and selling

DropToDocs does not sell, rent, or transfer your data to anyone. It does not use your data for advertising, credit decisions, or any purpose other than the feature described above. No person reads your data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

DropToDocs's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Sign out in the DropToDocs popup to revoke its access and remove your email address and token from your browser.
  • You can also remove its access at any time in your Google Account under Third-party apps & services.
  • Uninstalling the extension deletes everything it stored in your browser.
  • Documents it created stay in your Google Drive until you delete them.

Children

DropToDocs is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be noted in the extension's store listing.
