DropToDocs

Drop Office files in, get Google Docs, Sheets and Slides out. No Drive upload menus, no extra clicks.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store

How it works

  1. Click the DropToDocs icon in your browser toolbar and sign in with Google once.
  2. Choose or drag in one or more Word, Excel or PowerPoint files (.doc, .docx, .xls, .xlsx, .ppt, .pptx).
  3. Each file is converted to a Google Doc, Sheet or Slides presentation, saved in a Drive folder of your choice, and opens in a new tab, ready to edit.

Private by design

  • Files go straight from your browser to your own Google Drive. There are no DropToDocs servers.
  • It can only see files it created for you, nothing else in your Drive.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads.
